# Rights, research publication and restricted data

Author: Yudian Pan. The author explicitly approved this Working Paper and its public GitHub release on 13 October 2026. No additional MIT, Creative Commons or other blanket outbound grant has been selected; rights in owned text/code are reserved. Choosing an open license is not a precondition to a working paper. This notice does not license vendor observations or third-party work.

The public research package follows the restricted-data reproduction route recognized by the Journal of Finance (April 2024 Data and Code Sharing Policy) and JFQA (policy for submissions from January 2024): owned source code, input descriptions, acquisition route, source identifiers, and explicitly fictional demonstration data. Those journal policies are evidence of established practice, not licenses from any data provider and not proof that this project meets every requirement of those journals.

Digrin's terms effective October 1, 2026 were reviewed (https://www.digrin.com/terms/). Yahoo's data-provider notice prohibits republication/redistribution of LSEG content without written permission (https://in.help.yahoo.com/kb/SLN2310.html). No permission for bulk raw data, normalized return panels, benchmark paths or derivative datasets is presumed. CRSP/WRDS use is governed by an actual authorized account agreement; no CRSP data is included or represented as used. Public availability alone is insufficient.

The public research package excludes raw observations, normalized empirical panels, complete asset/strategy histories, figure source-data CSVs, standalone machine-readable figure SVGs, downloaded commercial/full-text publications and workbooks, private author metadata and correspondence. Original scholarly prose, research graphics and aggregate numerical findings are the contemplated academic publication, with source acknowledgment; they are not offered as a reusable vendor database. This bounded publication scope is distinct from licensing all intermediate derivatives. Anyone proposing a broader data distribution, commercial use or republication must assess the applicable source terms and rights separately. The private archive must never be uploaded wholesale.

Toolkits in the private archive retain upstream notices. academic-research-skills is CC BY-NC 4.0, nature-skills Apache 2.0, and the other four selected toolkits MIT, as verified at the archived pins. They are omitted from the public research package. No cvxgrp implementation was copied or relicensed; the independent engine cites the methodological reference. The synthetic core demo contains entirely fictional observations and no vendor history.

The project does not claim legal certification or a grant to redistribute every derived file. `docs/RELEASE_GATES.json` governs the actual outbound artifact; `docs/SOURCES_AND_RIGHTS.csv` is the source-specific record. Restricted raw data alone is not a reason to forbid academic publication.
